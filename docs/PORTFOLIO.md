## LinkedIn project entry

**Title:** Lunar Life — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026  
**Role:** Gameplay Programmer / Designer  
**Team:** Kareem Dahrouj, Aryan Patel, and Jimmy Lam  
**Project link:** https://github.com/Jjxmmy/lunar-life

**Description:**

Collaborated on a pixel-art educational game built with Godot 4. Players complete a five-day lunar-outpost mission by balancing power, greenhouse crops, science, and crew safety, with interactive lessons on astronaut training, space farming, and space weather. The solar-particle-event alert references an archived NASA CCMC DONKI record; exposure and resource systems are simplified simulations.

My contribution focused on interactive astronaut-training and space-farming gameplay, including Suit Up, Floaty Fix, planting and crew-health activities, lunar-regolith sorting, first harvest, and shelter preparation. I integrated these activities with mission tasks and progression.

**Relevant skills:** Godot, GDScript, gameplay programming, 2D game development, mission progression, interactive learning, data-driven design.
